Built for Businesses Like Yours

Every business has its own repetitive work. Here's how a small team of AI coworkers can fit into a few common ones.

A small law office

The owner's reality

  • Intake calls interrupt billable work all day, and most aren't a fit.
  • Hours go into first drafts of the same letters and agreements.
  • Court dates and deadlines live in someone's head or on a sticky note.
  • Time entries get rebuilt from memory on Friday, and billable hours slip away.

Your AI coworker team

  • Front Desk screens new inquiries 24/7 using the intake questions your attorneys approve, and books consults only for matters you take.
  • Legal & Paperwork drafts engagement letters, NDAs, and demand letters from your templates for attorney review.
  • Legal & Paperwork also summarizes depositions and long filings, and finds any passage across a case in plain English.
  • Operations tracks filing deadlines once an attorney confirms them, and reminds the team well ahead of time.
  • Bookkeeping turns emails and calendar events into draft time entries and sends invoices.

👉 More hours on billable work, fewer missed opportunities, and deadlines flagged well before they're due.

Common Questions

Will AI coworkers replace my staff?

They replace tasks, not people. The goal is to free your team to spend time with clients and on higher-value work, instead of hiring just to keep up with busywork.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

ChatGPT knows the internet. Your AI coworker knows your business. We build a knowledge base from your documents and processes, train your coworker on your workflows and tone, and connect it to your tools, so it actually does the work instead of just chatting about it.

What if it gets something wrong?

We start with you approving its work, review real conversations with you in the first weeks, and set it up to hand off to a person whenever it isn't sure.

Can it give legal, tax, or medical advice?

No. AI coworkers draft, organize, remind, and answer routine questions. The professional calls always stay with you and your licensed team.

Is my clients' information safe?

Each coworker only gets the access its job requires. Before launch, we walk you through exactly which tools it connects to and where data goes. For healthcare practices, we set it up to meet HIPAA requirements before it touches any patient information.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. If you can explain how you'd train a new hire, you can work with us. We handle the setup, connections, and ongoing tuning.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

Your first coworker can be live in as little as 14 days. Pricing depends on how many coworkers you need and what they connect to. We start with a free workflow review and give you a clear, fixed quote.
