PlayMap LA

Helping Parents Find the Right Activity in Under 3 Minutes

The Challenge

Parents have more information than ever, yet finding something great to do with their kids remains frustratingly difficult.

Events are scattered across Facebook groups, library calendars, city websites, newsletters, blogs, Instagram accounts, and ticketing platforms. Existing family event websites often feel cluttered, overwhelming, and difficult to navigate.

Parents weren't struggling because there weren't enough events. They were struggling because:

  • • Information was fragmented across multiple sources
  • • Important details were difficult to find
  • • Hyper-local events were often missing
  • • Free and paid activities lived on different platforms
  • • Discovery felt overwhelming rather than helpful
  • • Planning a simple family outing required opening multiple tabs

Most platforms focused on showing more events. Parents wanted help making a decision.

How can we help parents discover something great to do today in under 3 minutes?

Research & Validation

Before designing the platform, we spoke with local parents and conducted a small focus group to better understand how families discover activities today. The feedback was remarkably consistent.

Parents wanted:

  • • A cleaner experience
  • • Less scrolling
  • • Better filters
  • • Nearby recommendations
  • • Complete information upfront
  • • Help planning an entire outing, not just finding an event

Parents don't need more listings. They need more confidence in their decision.

The Solution

PlayMap LA was designed as a family discovery platform rather than a traditional event directory. Every design decision was guided by one principle: make family planning feel effortless.

A Clean, Parent-First Experience

Unlike many event websites that prioritize volume, PlayMap LA prioritizes clarity — reducing clutter so parents quickly find relevant options without feeling overwhelmed.

Everything in One Place

PlayMap LA combines free events, paid events, classes, family-friendly places, seasonal activities, community programs, and local attractions — giving families a single destination for discovery.

Quick Filters

Parents can instantly filter by age, location, activity type, date, and free vs paid — moving from hundreds of possibilities to a few relevant recommendations within seconds.

Near Me Discovery

Los Angeles is a city of neighborhoods. PlayMap LA includes Near Me search, zip code search, interactive map exploration, and localized recommendations — making it easy to discover nearby events, classes, and places.

Complete Information

Instead of forcing users to jump between websites, PlayMap surfaces location, pricing, age suitability, timing, event details, and nearby recommendations — helping parents make decisions faster.

AI-Powered Day Planning

Finding an event is only the first step. PlayMap LA uses AI-powered recommendations and deep-linking to transform a single activity into a complete family outing — what to do before, where to eat, and what to do after.

Content That Goes Beyond Lists

Guides include ready-to-use mini itineraries, family day plans, nearby attractions, suggested routes, and practical planning tips — helping parents spend less time researching and more time creating memories.

Community Features

Parent Match helps families turn solo outings into playdates — connecting parents with kids the same age who are planning to go at the same time, without ongoing group chats or spam.

Results

Within the first three months, PlayMap LA demonstrated strong early traction despite having no paid acquisition budget and minimal social media promotion.

Growth was driven primarily through:

  • • Word of mouth
  • • Search and SEO
  • • Community sharing
  • • User experience — rather than paid advertising
